  • TF-IDF (Term Frequency–Inverse Document Frequency) weighs each word by how distinctive it is — common words like "the" score near zero, while words that reliably separate real from fake news score high
  • Every cleaned article becomes a vector of 3,000 TF-IDF weights, one per vocabulary word
  • Logistic Regression — a supervised binary classifier — is trained to find a weight (coefficient) for each of those 3,000 features
  • Trained on 35,911 articles, validated on 8,978 unseen ones, reaching 98.73% accuracy
  • For a new article, the model multiplies each word's TF-IDF value by its learned coefficient, sums them with an intercept, and passes the result through a sigmoid function to output a Real/Fake probability — not just a guess
